A rhythm game for the M-VAVE FM-1.

⚠ Not yet tried on a real FM-1. It is tested in CI and in an FM-1 emulator only. Install it at your own risk.

Testers wanted. Have an FM-1? Try it and tell us how it went in an issue on GitHub.

I've always loved rhythm games. Countless hours with apps, in Japanese arcades and with Clone Hero, so this had to happen.

VaveHero takes this to the FM-1. Notes come down a neon highway, and you play them on the white keys while a synthwave band plays along, every sound made on the FM-1 itself.

An alpha: a proof of concept. Its eight songs are generated demo songs, made to try the game out. Real songs are welcome (how).

8lanes, on the white keys C4–C5
8songs, EASY to EXP
29synth sounds and drum kits

Install

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Connect the FM-1 directly with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  3. Close other MIDI apps, tick the box and press Install.

Loading the firmware…

Log

  

  
It takes about a minute: do not unplug the cable. If it stops, replug and press Install again.
    If the FM-1 will not start, hold OCT− while switching it on, and install again. To go back to the official
    firmware, use M-VAVE's updater M-UPGRADE; if its sounds look wrong afterwards, use Reset Patches in its System menu.
    To move on to another custom firmware, go back to the official one first.


  
Playing it

  

    
PLAYStart a song, or pause it. On the title screen, FX plays a demo.

    
SELECTPick a song. Each song has its own level.

    
In a songPlay the notes on the white keys. OCT− and OCT+ when the song changes octave, FX for
      OP BOOST, HOME to quit.

    
FREE SOLOWhere the highway lights up, every key is yours: play what you like.

    
KNOB 2–4Your sound, the speed for practice, and the mix.

    
ALGORITHMGRIT: how dirty the sound is.

    
GLOSPREAD: every other white key, for big fingers.

    
SEQAUTO: the song plays itself.

    
MIDI MODEThe last song in the list: the FM-1 as a sound module for your DAW.

Your songs in VaveHero

  
A song is an ordinary MIDI file: channel 1 is the part you play, the other channels are the band. Try yours in
    the browser version, then send it in an
    issue on GitHub. Your own songs only, not covers,
    and say that it may be shared under the GPL-3.0. How to make one:
